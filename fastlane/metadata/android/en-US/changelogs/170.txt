v0.17.0

- Completely removed H.264, RTSP, ONVIF, and legacy recording pipelines.
- Focused the entire app on ultra-low-latency MJPEG streaming with maximum image fidelity.
- Added configurable bitrate targets/limits (Unlimited, 20 Mbps, 12 Mbps, 8 Mbps, 4 Mbps, 2 Mbps, 1 Mbps).
- Added customizable JPEG compression quality slider (20% - 98%).
- Added comprehensive camera focus options: Continuous AF, Single AF/Trigger, Fixed/Infinity, Macro, and manual focus distance slider.
- Added interactive Tap-to-Focus on the web live stream canvas.
- Complete redesign of the web interface with modern responsive dark mode.
- Added toggle to hide stream preview in the web UI for reduced network and CPU load.
- Added dedicated quick-copy button and direct link for the MJPEG stream.
